Does adding a thread to a closed review reopen it too, or only a reply does?             32h ago

△  Both do. add and reply each go through send, and send opens the review
   first. A new thread in a closed review opens it, and starts a new round
   when nothing else waits, exactly as a reply does.

   The one difference is the thread. reply also sets its thread back to
   open, since you may be answering in a closed one. add makes a thread that
   is open from the start.
